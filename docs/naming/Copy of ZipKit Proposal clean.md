# The Situation

ZipKit already has real traction, active marketing, and a website that is producing leads. The issue is not whether the business is working. The issue is whether the website, tracking, and ownership structure are set up well enough to support what comes next.

Critical website infrastructure and access still need to be brought fully under ZipKit’s control. Lead tracking needs to be tightened so the source data is more useful inside Pipedrive and reporting is easier to trust. The site also needs a round of technical and content cleanup to improve visibility, reduce risk, and strengthen the foundation for future growth.

A recent crawl surfaced a broad pattern of avoidable issues, including missing meta descriptions, weak page titles, missing alt text, oversized images, broken internal links, low-content pages, no structured data, zero URLs in the XML sitemap, and live placeholder or duplicate content.

The immediate priority is to secure ownership and access, protect continuity during the transition, clean up the highest-priority issues, improve lead tracking, and establish a clear plan for ongoing improvement.

# Appendix

This appendix defines the terms used throughout these agreements so the scope is clear, expectations are aligned, and both parties understand what is and is not included.

## Website Terms

### Hosting transfer

Moving the website from the current hosting environment to a new hosting environment under your control or an approved provider’s control. This includes coordinating the move, preserving site functionality, and confirming the site resolves correctly after launch.

### DNS transfer

Moving or updating control of the domain’s Domain Name System so website traffic, email records, and related services point to where they should. DNS changes are planned and coordinated to avoid disruption, but they must be handled carefully because website and email records often live in the same DNS zone.

### Access recovery

Identifying, requesting, and organizing access to the accounts, credentials, licenses, and third-party services required to manage the website safely and independently.

### Security hardening

Applying baseline website security measures such as update review, login protection, credential cleanup, backup setup, and other standard protections that reduce avoidable risk.

### License cleanup

Reviewing plugin, theme, and tool licenses currently tied to the site, identifying which are vendor-controlled, and either transferring, replacing, renewing, or retiring them as needed.

### Placeholder cleanup

Removing or replacing visible placeholder, test, draft, duplicate, or unfinished content that weakens trust or creates confusion for visitors and search engines.

### About page update

Reworking the About page using your direction, current ownership, and team structure, approved messaging, and any photos or assets you provide.

### Microsoft Clarity

Installing and configuring Microsoft Clarity so the site can collect session recordings, heatmaps, and behavior data for website analysis and conversion improvement.

### Analytics review

Verifying that core measurement tools such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console are accessible, functioning, and connected properly enough to support reporting and troubleshooting.

### Gravity Forms integration

Connecting website forms built in Gravity Forms to downstream systems using a standard plugin or a connector-based configuration.

### Pipedrive connection

Connecting website form submissions to Pipedrive so agreed lead data can be sent into the customer relationship management system automatically.

### Field mapping

Matching each form field to the correct destination field in Pipedrive or another connected system so information lands in the right place.

### UTM tracking

Capturing available source data from tagged traffic and passing agreed fields into Pipedrive or reporting systems, provided campaign tagging and form field mapping are set up correctly.

### Source attribution

Preserving available lead-source information so form submissions can be associated with channels such as Google Ads, Meta, organic search, or other tagged traffic sources.

### Site health report

A plain-language document that identifies major technical, structural, content, and tracking issues on the website, prioritizes them by business impact, and recommends what to fix next.

### Roadmap

A prioritized sequence of recommended work based on risk, effort, and expected impact, so improvements are handled in a sensible order rather than randomly.

### Core updates

Routine updates to WordPress core files, plugins, and themes are intended to keep the site secure, stable, and compatible.

### Daily backups

Automatic copies of the website are taken regularly so the site can be restored more quickly if something breaks.

### Uptime monitoring

Automated checks that alert us when the website becomes unavailable or behaves like it may be failing.

### Minor edits

Small approved changes to existing website content, layout, links, images, or settings that do not require a redesign or major development effort.

### Optimization work

Incremental improvement work based on the approved roadmap, such as metadata updates, image cleanup, broken-link repair, content refinement, internal linking, or similar items.

### Monthly summary

A plain-language update that explains what was done, what was found, what changed, and what should happen next.

### Project board

A shared task board is used to track open work, priorities, status, and notes, so there is visibility into what is being worked on.

### Included hours

The monthly block of time included in the website management fee for maintenance, approved minor edits, and roadmap-based improvement work.

### Overage

Work performed beyond the included monthly time or outside the agreed scope, billed at the approved hourly rate.

## Marketing Terms

### Strategy audit

An upfront research and planning phase that evaluates your market position, search visibility, competitors, content opportunities, and growth priorities before ongoing strategy work begins.

### Competitive review

A structured review of selected competitors and relevant search competitors to understand how they position themselves, what they rank for, where they appear stronger, and where opportunity exists.

### Keyword research

Research into the search terms and topic clusters most relevant to your products, locations, customer intent, and growth goals.

### Search intent

The reason behind a search query, such as learning, comparing, evaluating, or buying. This helps determine what kind of page or content should exist.

### SEO baseline

A snapshot of current search visibility, content structure, metadata, and ranking opportunity at the beginning of the engagement.

### Content gap review

A review of where your site lacks pages, topics, depth, or structure compared to what users search for and what competitors are covering.

### Site architecture review

A review of how pages are organized, grouped, and connected so the site makes sense to users and search engines.

### Priority pages

The pages that should be created, expanded, or improved first because they are most likely to support visibility, conversion, or business goals.

### City pages

Pages built for specific cities or local markets to support location-based search visibility and local relevance.

### State pages

Pages built for specific states or broader geographic targets where search demand and expansion plans justify that coverage.

### Strategic partnership

Ongoing advisory work focused on market awareness, search opportunity, content direction, conversion improvement, and growth planning rather than one-off task execution.

### Trend review

Ongoing review of changes in search behavior, competitor movement, customer language, and emerging opportunities that may affect marketing priorities.

### Content planning

Recommending what content or pages should be produced, in what order, for what audience, and for what search or conversion purpose.

### Conversion path

The steps a visitor takes from first click to inquiry, lead, or sale, including the pages, forms, and calls to action that influence that movement.

### Execution oversight

Reviewing, directing, or advising on implementation work so page builds, copy, campaigns, or related assets stay aligned with the strategy.

### Deliverables

Any written findings, recommendations, planning documents, or research outputs created as part of the engagement.

### No guarantee

Search rankings, traffic volume, and lead volume depend on many variables outside direct control. Strategy improves the odds. It does not create a guaranteed result.
